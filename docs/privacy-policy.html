Get Up Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Get Up app for Android (the "Application"), developed and operated by Atanu Shuvam Roy (the "Service Provider"). This policy describes, plainly, what data the Application handles and why.

Summary

Get Up is designed to work entirely on your device. Your timer settings, exercise routines, theme preference, and activity history (completed/skipped tasks for the last 30 days) are stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. The Service Provider does not operate a backend server for this data, and none of it is transmitted anywhere by the Application.

Information the Application Stores Locally
  • Timer configuration: work interval, exercise window, exercise list, sound mode, theme, and overlay preference
  • Timer state: current countdown, cycle count, completed/skipped task counters
  • A rolling 30-day log of completed and skipped tasks, used only to render the in-app 7-day activity chart
  • Onboarding flags (e.g. whether you've seen the notification/overlay permission screens)

None of this data leaves your device. Uninstalling the Application removes it, since it is stored in the app's private storage.

Permissions the Application Requests
  • Notifications — to alert you when it's time to work, exercise, or drink water.
  • Display over other apps — to show the optional floating timer overlay when you leave the app.
  • Foreground service — to keep the timer running accurately in the background.
  • Battery optimization exemption — optional, requested so background timers aren't delayed by the system.
  • Boot completed — to resume a running timer after your device restarts.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit personal data; they exist solely to make the timer, alerts, and overlay function correctly.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following Google Play services to support specific features:

  • Google Play Billing — used only for the optional, one-time "Support Development" donation. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play; the Application does not see or store your payment details.
  • Google Play In-App Review & In-App Update — used to prompt for an app store review and to check for app updates through the Play Store.

The Application does not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs. There are no ads and no third-party trackers in this app.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect personal information at all beyond what is described above.

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is retained locally until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. The rolling activity log automatically discards entries older than 30 days. You can delete all locally stored data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Get Up → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Your Rights

Because Get Up does not collect or store your data outside of your own device, there is no remote copy of your data for the Service Provider to access, export, or delete on your behalf. If you have questions about this, or about how Google Play Billing/Play Core handle data on Google's side, contact the Service Provider at atanuroy911@gmail.com.

Security

Since the Application does not transmit your personal data to a server it operates, the primary safeguard is that your data stays on your device, protected by your device's own security (screen lock, storage encryption, etc.), as provided by Android.
